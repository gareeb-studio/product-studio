❓ Quick questions

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining is free. If a product needs money later, that's decided by vote first.

I don't know coding. Can I still join?

Yes. Products need ideas, research, design, testing, marketing and sales — not only coding.

What happens after I send the form?

Co-owners vote on your request page. If you pass, you get an invitation to the team as a co-owner, and then you can vote on the next person.

Why GitHub?

So every idea, vote and piece of work is saved in one place that nobody can quietly change.
