The Entry Agreement — you tick this in your Join Request

  • ☐ I have read and accept the Governance (phases, gates, locked phases).
  • ☐ I accept voting: ⅔ for normal gates & admissions, 75% for major decisions.
  • ☐ I understand contribution points ≠ ownership ≠ voting power — ownership is agreed separately, in writing, before development on a product begins.
  • ☐ I accept: one active product at a time, and no skipping phases.
  • ☐ I will record ideas, decisions, votes and work in GitHub — not only in chat.
  • ☐ I will not push directly to the protected main branch.

What happens after you click Join

Step 1 — Request

GitHub opens a pre-filled Join Request. Fill in who you are, what you bring, and tick the Entry Agreement. Your GitHub username is the author — that's your identity.

Step 2 — Vote

Current members comment VOTE: ADMIT or VOTE: REJECT on your request before the deadline. Result stays recorded forever.

Step 3 — Merged = Member

If it passes, a PR adds you to members.json + MEMBERS.md and records the vote in votes/. When that PR merges, you are a member — and you get a vote on the next person.
